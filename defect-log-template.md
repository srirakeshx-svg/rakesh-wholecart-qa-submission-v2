## Severity guide

- **Critical:** A major business workflow is blocked, or there is a severe
  integrity/security impact with no reasonable workaround.
- **High:** Important functionality is unusable or produces a materially
  incorrect outcome; a workaround is difficult or unavailable.
- **Medium:** Functionality is impaired, but the workflow can continue with a
  reasonable workaround.
- **Low:** Minor usability, content, or visual issue with little impact on
  completing the workflow.

Choose severity based on demonstrated impact, not on how surprising the issue
seems. If impact is uncertain, note that uncertainty.
